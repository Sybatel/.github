# Sybatel Code of Conduct

Sybatel Technologies is committed to maintaining professional, respectful, inclusive, and constructive spaces for technical collaboration.

This Code of Conduct applies to Sybatel-managed repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other public collaboration spaces.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and respectfully;
- critique ideas, designs, and implementations rather than individuals;
- welcome good-faith questions, feedback, and contributions;
- respect different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives;
- protect privacy, confidentiality, security-sensitive information, and intellectual property;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest where appropriate;
- follow repository-specific technical, security, licensing, and contribution requirements;
- act in ways that support a safe, reliable, and productive engineering community.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory, demeaning, or exclusionary language or conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- impersonation, spam, manipulation, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- knowingly submitting malicious code, deceptive contributions, or harmful content;
- exposing credentials, confidential information, personal data, or security-sensitive material in public channels;
- repeated bad-faith participation after a maintainer has provided direction.

## Enforcement

Sybatel maintainers may moderate content and participation when this policy is violated.

Depending on severity, context, and recurrence, actions may include:

1. clarification or a private warning;
2. removal or editing of comments, issues, discussions, or pull requests;
3. temporary participation restrictions;
4. permanent blocking from Sybatel-managed collaboration spaces;
5. escalation through appropriate organizational, contractual, or legal channels when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting the integrity of Sybatel-managed spaces.

## Reporting concerns

If a conduct concern should be reviewed privately, contact Sybatel through:

https://sybatel.com/contact

Please provide enough context for the matter to be reviewed fairly, including relevant links or screenshots where available.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Information will be shared only as reasonably necessary to review and address the matter.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to public Sybatel-managed collaboration spaces and to individuals acting as representatives of Sybatel in those spaces.

A repository or product may publish additional requirements. Where that occurs, the more specific policy applies alongside this organization-level Code of Conduct.

## Good-faith participation

Disagreement about technical decisions, product direction, architecture, implementation, or policy is not itself a violation of this Code of Conduct. Constructive debate is welcome when it remains professional, relevant, and respectful.
