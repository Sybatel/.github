# Security Policy

Sybatel Technologies takes the security of its software, infrastructure, and users seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for suspected security vulnerabilities.

Instead, report the issue privately through the Sybatel contact channel:

- https://sybatel.com/contact

Please include:

- the affected product, repository, or URL;
- a clear description of the vulnerability;
- steps to reproduce the issue;
- relevant screenshots, logs, or proof-of-concept details;
- your preferred contact information for follow-up.

Use **Security Vulnerability Report** as the subject or opening line where possible.

## Responsible disclosure

We ask security researchers and users to:

- avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that does not belong to them;
- avoid actions that could degrade service availability;
- avoid social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, or denial-of-service testing;
- allow Sybatel a reasonable period to investigate and remediate a reported issue before public disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies to Sybatel-owned public repositories and publicly accessible Sybatel systems unless a product or repository publishes a more specific security policy.

Private or customer-specific systems may be governed by separate contractual or security requirements.

## Response

Sybatel will review credible vulnerability reports, assess severity and impact, and coordinate remediation as appropriate. Response times may vary by product, severity, and operational context.

Thank you for helping us keep Sybatel systems secure.
